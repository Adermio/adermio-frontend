Subscription purchased on adermio.com

  1. Click the button below and enter the email address of your subscription.
  2. Open the sign-in link you receive by email.

Manage or cancel my subscription

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period: you keep your Premium access until then, after which no further payment is taken. A confirmation is sent to you by email. On the same page, you can also find your invoices and change your card.

Subscription purchased in the app (iPhone)

Subscriptions bought in the app are managed by Apple:

  • iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Adermio Premium → Cancel Subscription
  • Or directly via apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
