Our mission

Democratizing access to skin expertise.

We created Adermio to bridge the gap between everyday skin problems and the right answers, which are often hard to find.

Our story

Why Adermio?

Acne affects 80% of teenagers and 40% of adults. Yet getting reliable, personalized advice remains complex, and medical waiting times can be long.

Faced with this wait, many turn to unsuitable solutions on the internet or pile up expensive products without results. Adermio was born from this observation.

Our goal is to offer an instant, reliable and objective analysis. Using computer vision, we analyze your skin to guide you toward the right routine.

Our commitment

Adermio does not sell any cosmetic products. We are not affiliated with any brand. Our recommendations are 100% neutral and come from our database of dermatologically reliable products.

Our values

An ethical approach

We believe technology should serve your well-being, with complete transparency.

100% independent

We refuse all commercial partnerships. Unlike influencers or magazines, our recommendations are based solely on ingredient efficacy, not marketing.

Total privacy

Your face is sensitive data. Your photos are encrypted, analyzed by AI, then anonymized. Your identity remains protected, and data is never resold.

Kindness & support

Acne has a strong psychological impact. Our tone is always encouraging, never guilt-inducing. We are here to help you.
